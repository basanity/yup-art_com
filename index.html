About the work

A painting can return us to the way the world once felt. Memory transforms a moment. Light grows brighter, colors deepen, perspective shifts, and certain details take on greater meaning. These memories live, breathe, and vibrate within us. Through texture, light, and color, painting brings them before our eyes again, fresh and alive, even when the moments belong to a past we never experienced ourselves.

The artist

Yuliya Popkova

Yuliya Popkova is a Florida-based oil painter whose work is filled with color, atmosphere, and emotional depth.

Yuliya is an active member of the Central Brevard Art Association and regularly exhibits her work in local shows and group exhibitions. She continues to refine her craft through advanced study, workshops, and a deep personal interest in color theory and composition. Her artistic style evolves as she explores new techniques and creative directions.
